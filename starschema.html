The OLAP Cube

An OLAP Cube stores data multi-dimensionally. Unlike a flat spreadsheet, you can "rotate" it to see sales by Date, then by Region, then by Product instantly.

Think of it this way: A cell in the cube might represent [January] + [New York] + [Laptops] = $50,000.
